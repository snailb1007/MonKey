## Cross-Phase E2E Flow Integration

- **Flow 1: Diagnostic Discovery & Doctor Flow**
  `monkey doctor` -> `find_monka_device_sets` -> Interface A/B inspection -> OS TCC / udev guidance. All components wire cleanly without friction.
- **Flow 2: Device Probing & Capability Negotiation**
  `monkey info` and `monkey probe` query USB descriptors and feature report capabilities, falling back safely on mock transports.
- **Flow 3: Synthetic & Hardware Benchmarking**
  `monkey bench` accurately exercises bulk and feature channels with zero-copy packet codecs, measuring throughput and percentile latencies.
- **Flow 4: LCD Rendering & Streaming Pipeline**
  `monkey lcd image/anim/test-pattern` encodes 128x128 RGB565 frames into 8 chunks of 4096 bytes and streams over Interface A with enforced 10-25ms pacing.
- **Flow 5: Ambient RGB & Profile Management**
  `monkey rgb set/save/restore/status` cleanly decouples 30Hz volatile RAM updates from debounced 500ms flash commits, gating wireless writes on battery level.
